# The hall

`st-pauls-hall.wav`: the reverberation of St Paul's Hall, Huddersfield (RT60 2.1 s), from the MAIR library
(Hyunkook Lee and Connor Millns, Applied Psychoacoustics Lab, University of Huddersfield,
https://github.com/APL-Huddersfield/MAIR-Library-and-Renderer), licensed CC BY 4.0.

Made from the Hamasaki ambience array 01 (figure-of-eight microphones 7 m from the stage, the hall without the direct
sound): left = front-left + 0.6 × rear-left, right likewise; started just before the first arrival; decay eased from
2.1 s to about 1.6 s (a practice room under a singer); 2.2 s long with a 0.2 s fade; 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo.
The app loads it into the piano's reverb in place of the generated room, at the same overall level.
