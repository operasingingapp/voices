## Unsure / minor (not counted as problems)

- Where Mimì lives. S7 says she lives "in a white room above the roofs"; the Act 1 summary and A-mimi-1 say "under the roofs". The score has "Vivo sola, soletta, là in una bianca cameretta: guardo sui tetti e in cielo" (p. 72): she looks out over the roofs and at the sky. Neither phrase is literal, and the two disagree slightly. To match the score: "in a little white room, looking out over the roofs and the sky".
- Act 4 summary: "drifts off to sleep while Musetta prays". In the score Mimì falls asleep first (p. 273, end of S29), and Musetta's prayer starts after that (p. 274, S30). "While" could mean "while she sleeps", so it is not wrong, but "falls asleep; Musetta prays over her" would follow the order more closely.
- A-colline-1: "the bundle of books he could not pawn". The score has him throw a bundle of books tied in a handkerchief onto the table (p. 13) and complain that no pledges are taken on Christmas Eve (p. 14). It never says the books were what he tried to pawn. This is a reasonable inference, but it is not printed.
- A-colline-1: "is told to get a shave". Marcello says "Orso, ravviati il pelo" ("bear, tidy your fur", p. 50), and Colline is the one who brings up the barber. "Told to tidy himself up for a barber" would be closer, but the meaning is fair.
