# Check 1: running order sections (La bohème), checked against the print

Method: every joint was checked on the page image (pp. 20, 33, 54, 69, 75, 77-78, 110-111, 140, 145, 176, 179, 198, 209, 223, 228, 231, 247, 261, 269), the other joints and the singers of each section were checked page by page through the map and on the page where an entry was small or doubtful. Titles were compared with the printed words, zoomed where accents or punctuation mattered.

## Problems

None found. Specifically:
- Joints: every section starts where the draft says (page, system, bar, figure). Each section ends exactly where the next one starts, with no gap and no overlap, and the act boundaries are at pp. 82/83, 166/167 and 222/223.
- Titles: all 30 match the first sung words as printed. Checked in particular: "Si. Mi" on p. 69 sys 3 bar 3 (plain "Si.", no grave accent, confirmed at zoom); "In un coupè?" (Marcello does sing first on p. 223, before Rodolfo's "Con pariglia"); "Si può?.."; "O Mimì tu più non torni" (no comma, with the aside's opening parenthesis, p. 228); "Dunque è proprio finita!"; "Mi-mì?!".
- Sings and on stage: all lists agree with the print, including the small and offstage entries: Mimì's "Ah!" at (30); Marcello's offstage "trovò la poesia" in S9 (only Marcello: Schaunard and Colline finish on p. 77); Parpignol offstage in S11; Musetta offstage in S16; the Sergente silent but on stage in S16 (he enters p. 173) and singing "Eccola." and "Passi!" in S17; the Doganiere's "Vengo!" and "Vuoto!"; Musetta's laugh in S20 (p. 205), which has no notes and so is correctly not counted; Alcindoro singing up to his exit on p. 143 (S14), then silent when he comes back on p. 166 (S15, on stage); Colline's whispered "Come va?" and his "Musetta, a voi!" in S30.
- Set pieces: Che gelida manina (30) p. 64, Si. Mi chiamano Mimì (35) p. 69, O soave fanciulla (41) p. 78, Quando me'n vo' (21) p. 130, Donde lieta uscì (in S21 from (26) p. 206), the quartet (in S22 from (29) p. 209), O Mimì tu più non torni p. 228, Vecchia zimarra (19) p. 258 and Sono andati? ## Unsure (judgement calls, not errors)

1. S13/S14, p. 140. S14 starts at the break in the waltz (sys 2), but the waltz melody comes back at I.º TEMPO (sys 4) with Marcello's "Gioventù mia" and Musetta's "Sciogli, slaccia" over it, the climax of the waltz ensemble. A singer practising "Quando me'n vo'" (Marcello in particular) loses that return. The joint itself is clean, but consider whether the waltz piece should run on through the reprise.
2. S4/S5, p. 54. The stage direction for Rodolfo closing the door, sitting down and starting to write is printed over sys 1. That system is in S4, because S5 starts at (25) in sys 2. Open question 7 gives "so that his solitude belongs to the scene" as the reason for this joint. The double bar at the end of p. 53 (change to 2/4) would also be a clean joint and would carry that direction into S5.
3. S25/S26, p. 247. "(si spalanca l'uscio ed entra Musetta…)" is printed over sys 3 bar 3, the last bar of S25. Marcello's "Mu-" and the start of S26 come in bar 4. The draft's "the door bursts open" at the S26 joint is a bar late by the print, though the musical joint (the upbeat bar) is right.
