# Check 3: cast, chorus groups, section titles, open questions

Checked against: Ricordi vocal score (vs.pdf, 300 dpi page images, zoomed where needed), the page maps, and Castel's libretto (castel.pdf; its cast list is on printed p. 3, PDF p. 2).

## 4. OPEN QUESTIONS

1. **No cast list.** Confirmed: p. 1 is the title heading plus the music of Quadro Primo. The clef statements are confirmed (see section 1). Castel's list matches the draft: Benoit "bass (or tenor)", Alcindoro "bass (or tenor)", Parpignol tenor, Sergeant bass. Castel does not list the Doganiere, though he sings.
2. **Benoit or Benoît.** The print has "BENOIT" (p. 33) and "Beno-it!" (pp. 33, 39), with no circumflex. Castel also has "Benoit". I recommend keeping "Benoit". Mentioning "Benoît" as the usual French form would be optional.
3. **"Si. Mi chiamano Mimì".** Confirmed at 3× zoom: the print has "Si." with no accent and a period after it (p. 69 sys 3, "con semplicità"). It is a misprint for "Sì": the word means "yes", and Castel prints "Sì, mi chiamano Mimì," (libretto p. 36). Since titles follow the print, "Si." is defensible. Either way, the title and the synopsis/arcs ("Sì, mi chiamano Mimì") differ, so the app should pick one deliberately. I would correct it to "Sì. Mi chiamano Mimì" and note the misprint.
4. **"Ah!" in Che gelida manina.** Confirmed: Mimì's "Ah!" (sorpresa) is in bar 1 of (30), AND.no AFFETTUOSO (p. 64), and Rodolfo starts in bar 3. Listing Mimì as singing and leaving her "Ah!" out of the title are both right.
5. **S1/S2 joint (p. 20).** Confirmed: Marcello and Colline sing "-tor!....." in the bar marked (10) ALLEGRO, and "f deciso" comes in the next bar. The draft's choice is correct.
6. **Start of S3 (p. 33).** Confirmed:
   - There is a double bar and 2/4 at sys 1 bar 3.
   - Benoit's "Si può?.." is in sys 2.
   - "Beno-" stands before the double bar into ALL.º VIVO 6/8 at (17), and "-it!" comes after it.
   - So the start must come before (17), and the 2/4 double bar is a clean place.
7. **S5 start (p. 54).** Confirmed: (25) is marked "ALLEGRETTO" after a double bar with a change of key, and Rodolfo is alone, "(scrive, s'interrompe...)". The LENTO comes later, before "Chi è là!?". Both joints are clean, and the choice is reasonable.
8. **Short S8.** Confirmed: (41) LARGO SOSTENUTO opens with Rodolfo's "O soave fanciulla" in bar 1, and Marcello's offstage "trovò la poesia" falls in bar 2. That makes (41) the clean start of the duet, so it is right to keep S8 separate. Marcello is correctly listed in S9.
9. **Musetta's entrance has no section of its own.** The map shows Marcello's "Ch'io beva del" (p. 115, end of sys 2) and "tossico!" (p. 116, at (16)) on either side of the double bar. The same holds for Rodolfo's "Questa è Mimì" across (10) on p. 103. This agrees with the draft, but I checked it on the map only, not on the image.
10. **S14 start (p. 140).** Confirmed: there is no double bar at Musetta's "Ahi!". The marking is "strillando, quasi a piacere" with the piano marked "col canto" (no notes). The next double bar comes after the "a tempo / cres. molto" run. Starting at "Ahi!" is the only sensible joint.
11. **"ie guardie" (p. 169).** Confirmed: the type of the "l" is broken. The reading "le" is certain.
12. **S18 start (p. 179).** Confirmed: MARCELLO "(esce dal Cabaré)" sings "Mi-" (sorpreso) in the bar before the double bar of (8) ALLEGRO, and "-mì?!" falls in the first ALLEGRO bar. The draft's start is correct. I did not image-check the ANDANTE at (9) on p. 181.
13. **S20 start (p. 198).** Confirmed: "tristamente", Rodolfo's upbeat "Mi-" is in the last bar before the double bar and the new key, and LENTO TRISTE at (21) begins on p. 199 with "-mì è tanto malata!".
14. **S21 start (p. 206).** Confirmed:
   - (26) LENTO MOLTO starts the page.
   - Rodolfo's "Vai?" is in bar 1.
   - Mimì's "Donde lieta uscì" begins in bar 3.
   - The ANDANTINO comes later, at "Mimì al solitario".
   - The ending of S20 (p. 205, "Addio." / "Che!") matches the map.
15. **S22 start (p. 209).** Confirmed. Mimì's "addio senza ran-" is in the bar marked "(corta)" before (29), and her "-cor....." falls on the first beat of (29) "a tempo". Rodolfo enters later in that bar with "Dunque è proprio finita!". His "Addio sogni d'a-" runs to the end of the page. The elision is unavoidable, so accepting it is right.
16. **Quartet title.** The first sung words of the section are Rodolfo's "Dunque è proprio finita!". The title as it stands follows the rule the draft uses everywhere else, so I would keep it. Adding "… Addio dolce svegliare" would help users find the quartet, but it is an editorial choice, not a correction.
17. **"O Mimì tu più non torni".** Confirmed: the print reads "(O Mimì tu più non torni, O giorni belli", with an opening parenthesis, no comma after "Mimì", a comma after "torni", and a capital "O" in "O giorni" (p. 228). The title matches the print. The text for readers ("O Mimì, tu più non torni") is a reasonable normalisation. I would make them consistent only if the app's rule is "titles follow the print everywhere"; there is no error either way.
18. **S25 start (p. 231 sys 3).** Confirmed: "Che ora sia?" is sung over a chord tied from the bar before, and "mosso" follows in the next bar.
19. **S26 start.** Confirmed: Musetta enters at "ALL.º MOD.to AGITATO", Marcello's "-setta!" is under the fermata at the start of that system, and his "Mu-" falls just before the double bar (p. 247). The draft is correct.
20. **S27 extent.** Confirmed: Colline's "E tu... lasciali soli là!.." leads into Schaunard's "Filosofo, ragioni! È ver!.. Vo via!" and then (20) AND.no MOSSO, where Schaunard takes the water bottle (pp. 260–261). Keeping it in S27 is right.
21. **Small solo lines.** Both exist as printed: "UN VENDITORE AMBULANTE" ("VEND. AMB.", p. 92, tenor clef) and "RAGAZZO SOLO" (p. 109, treble clef, "Vo' la tromba, il cavallin!..."). Each has one tiny line. Leaving them out as characters is consistent with the Figaro model, and I agree.
22. **Doganiere.** Confirmed: "UN DOGANIERE" on p. 170, "DOGANIERE" on pp. 173 and 178. The stage directions speak of "i doganieri" in the plural, and on p. 173 the sergeant is named separately ("il sergente dei doganieri"). Nothing in the print says these are three different men, so one character is fine.
23. **Musetta offstage in S16.** Confirmed: "MUSETTA (dal Cabaré)" sings the vocalise and "Se nel bicchiere sta il piacer..." on p. 172. It is right to count her as singing in S16.
24. **Lengths.** I have no evidence to add.
25. **Act 4 setting.** Confirmed: p. 223 gives only "IN SOFFITTA (Come nel Quadro Primo)". The Act 3 set text on p. 167 says "È il febbraio, al finire; la neve è dappertutto." "Some time later" is fair wording.
