Method: compared every claim in STORY, Act 1-4 summaries, S1-S30 "what happens", CHARACTER ARCS, WHEN lines and CAST descriptions against the sung text and stage directions (map used for navigation; pages checked in the score images where it mattered: p. 69, 167, 179, among others).

## Problems

1. Act 3 summary, bullet 3: "Mimì ... asks Rodolfo to send back her few things".
   Score p. 207-208: "Le poche robe aduna che lasciai sparse ... Involgi tutto quanto in un grembiale e manderò il portiere" - he is to gather her things into an apron and she will send the porter to fetch them. Rodolfo does not send them back. (S21 has it right.)
   Fix: "asks Rodolfo to gather up her few things for the porter to collect, but to keep the bonnet if he likes".

## Unsure (not counted as problems)

- STORY: "within the hour they are in love". The score gives no time; it is a harmless gloss but not from the score. Could read "that same night they fall in love".
- S13: "he ... cannot hide that he is dying for her". Musetta sings "le angoscie tue non le vuoi dir ... ma ti senti morir" (p. 134): he will not speak his anguish, but he feels he is dying. "cannot hide" slightly shifts "won't say"; acceptable paraphrase, change only if you want it literal ("he will not admit his anguish, but he is dying inside").
- Act 3 summary, bullet 2: "Musetta laughing with a customer". At that moment Marcello only asks "Con chi ride?" (p. 205); the "signore" she was flirting with is named only in the quartet (p. 213-214). Fine as a summary.
