Order of the singers' staves (top to bottom) in the Breitkopf vocal score (Edition Breitkopf 1716), per piece.
"sys N" = Nth system from the top of the printed page. "Shared" = the singers take turns on ONE Italian sung staff
(full names printed above each entry); in all these recitatives that staff is followed by a German duplicate staff
(same music, German words) and then the piano, and no singer ever gets a staff of their own.

mozart-basilio-in-traccia-tosto: shared (Il Conte, Susanna, Cherubino, Basilio all on one Italian staff, German duplicate staff below; pages 72-74, every system). No separate singer staves.
mozart-bravo-che-bella-voce: shared (La Contessa, Susanna, Cherubino on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 106 sys 5 - 108). No separate singer staves.
mozart-cinque-dieci-venti: Susanna, Figaro (page 9, sys 4: "S." over "Figaro"; same on page 10 sys 1-3, page 11 sys 3-4, page 13 sys 1-3). Notes: pages 8 sys 5 - 9 sys 3 have only Figaro's staff ("F."); on page 9 sys 3 Susanna enters on that F. staff (clef change) before getting her own staff in sys 4. Some later systems are one-staff again (page 10 sys 4 "F." with Susanna entering; page 11 sys 1 "S." with Figaro entering, sys 2 "F."). Whenever there are two staves, S. is above F.
mozart-cos-e-questa-commedia: shared (Il Conte, Figaro, Susanna and the "Tutti" Evviva (our Coro) all on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 76 sys 3 - 79 sys 2). No separate singer staves. (The "Giovani liete" chorus on page 79 sys 3 is a separate CORO staff pair, but it follows the recitative and is not in our score.)
mozart-cosa-stai-misurando: shared (Susanna, Figaro on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 13 sys 4 - 15). No separate singer staves.
mozart-ed-aspettaste-il-giorno: shared (Bartolo, Marcellina on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 30-31). No separate singer staves. Bartolo sings first.
mozart-esci-omai-garzon-malnato: La Contessa, Il Conte (page 142, sys 4: "Gn." over "Gf."; same on pages 143-147, e.g. 143 sys 1-4, 145 sys 2-4, 146 sys 1-4, 147 sys 1). Notes: pages 141 sys 1 - 142 sys 3 use ONE shared staff whose label switches (Graf / Gf. / Gn.); page 144 sys 3 - 145 sys 1 they again share one staff (labelled Gf., then Gn.) before splitting back to Gn. over Gf.; page 147 sys 2 (Scena IX, "Susanna!") is again one shared staff. The order Gn. above Gf. never flips.
mozart-evviva: shared (Figaro, Susanna, Basilio, Cherubino, Il Conte on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 81 sys 2 - 83). No separate singer staves. (On page 82 sys 4 Susanna and Figaro sing "Ah! fin domani sol..." together as two-part notes on that one staff.)
mozart-or-bene-ascolta-e-taci: shared (Susanna, Figaro on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 21-24). No separate singer staves.
mozart-quante-buffonerie: shared (La Contessa, Susanna, Cherubino, Il Conte on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 114-119 sys 3). No separate singer staves.
mozart-quanto-duolmi-susanna: shared (La Contessa, Susanna, Cherubino on one Italian staff, German staff below; pages 101-103 sys 2). No separate singer staves.
mozart-se-a-caso-madama: Susanna, Figaro (page 18, sys 3: "S." over "F."; same on page 18 sys 4, page 19 sys 5, page 20 sys 1-3).
