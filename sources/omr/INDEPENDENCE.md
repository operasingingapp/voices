# Independence rules for every check (user requirement)

- Each transcriber and checker works only from the printed PDF and the file under check. Never pass on earlier
  findings, "known and accepted" spots, or where mistakes were found before.
- Two transcriptions are made separately; neither sees the other or the scanning app's XML.
- A disputed spot goes to a NEW helper who sees only the page and the question "what does the print show here?",
  not the competing readings. Its reading is followed.
- Any spot where the builder overrides a checker is listed in the number's notes for the user.
- The final pass on every number is a fresh check (FRESHCHECK.md) with no notes at all.
