## Print errors and judgement calls
- Recit Quante buffonerie, bar 54: printed half a beat too long (Conte "la-" printed as a quarter without flag). Read as an
  eighth, so La Contessa's "A voi..." enters on the 4th eighth.
- Recit Quanto duolmi, bar 11: "a-vanti,a-van-ti" on five notes: sung a-van-ti‿a-van-ti (elision on the third note).
- Recit Quante buffonerie, bar 25: "spia-cea": "cea" is one syllable on one note.
- Page 118 has four systems; the scan's system finder missed the third one; it was added by hand (sys78b).
- Recit Quante buffonerie, bar 32 → 33: the print draws arcs from all chord notes to the end of the system, but at the
  next system only A4 and C5 continue at the same pitch (Eb4 → D4, F → F#). Only A4 and C5 are tied.
- Recit Bravo, bar 8: "sta-tu-ra..." without accent (the mark over the a is a speck; settled by an independent judge).
- Recit Quanto duolmi, bar 5: "sa-i..." without accent (the mark is a speck).
- No. 12, bar 40 (file numbering; the pickup is bar 1), piano LH: the chord A2-C-G is printed without a sharp on C,
  although the same chord in bar 50 has C#. Kept as printed (C natural); probably an engraver's omission.
- No. 12: the opening one-eighth upbeat is the file's bar 1 (implicit bar), so file bar = printed bar + 1.
- No. 11, bars 53→54, 55→56, 57→58: the print draws an arc from each chord note; same-pitch notes are tied and one
  slur joins the chords.
