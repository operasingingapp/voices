# Recitativo "Che sussurro! che strepito!", Peters pp. 109-112
SEGS `1/4/4/0/` · SINGERS `A:Don Alfonso:D.A.:F/4,D:Dorabella:Do.:G/2,F:Fiordiligi:Fi.:G/2,E:Despina:De.:G/2,R:Ferrando:Fe.:G/2/-1,G:Guglielmo:G.:F/4`
**Bars:** 43, in two chunks: p. 109 system 3 to p. 110 (bars 1-22) and p. 111 to the double bar on p. 112 system 2 (bars 23-43).
**Layout:** "Allegretto" stands at bar 11 beat 3 and "sempre a tempo colla parte" at bar 32; both are in the score as words. The piece ends on Dorabella's "che fac-"; the word is completed in the first bar of No. 14.
**Process:** each chunk had two readers and a judge (first chunk 0 X / 3 Y; second 6 X / 2 Y).
**Print oddities:** bars 28 (Guglielmo, "A-mor...") and 31 (Ferrando) are each printed an eighth too long. The D3 "A-" in bar 28 and the second C ("qui") in bar 31 are shortened to eighths; which note the engraver got wrong cannot be proved.
**Italian rhythm:** used in bars 2, 15, 17, 21, 22, 25, 28, 34.
**Fresh check:** one round, one error, fixed. Bar 33, piano: the E on the fourth beat is E flat in all three octaves (no natural is printed).
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters ("perchè", "si possente" without accent).
**Tempo:** quarter = 92. Estimated; no separate track timing was found for this recitative.
