# No. 15a Aria "Rivolgete a lui lo sguardo" (KV 584), Peters pp. 125-134
SEGS `1/4/4/2/Allegro;129/2/2/2/Allegro molto` · SINGERS `G:Guglielmo:G.:F/4`
**Bars:** 195, in three chunks:
- pp. 125-128: bars 1-75
- pp. 129-131: bars 76-137 (Allegro molto, cut time, from bar 129; fermatas in bars 107, 109, 128)
- p. 132 to p. 134 system 2: bars 138-195 (bars 191-195 have no voice staff; fermata in bar 195)

**Process:** each chunk had two readers, a judge and a marks sweep, then stitched.
**Kept as printed:** "con-fron-te" (libretto "confronto"), "usignuolo", "Canada" without accent, "Pich ne" without hyphen (bar 101), "da." without accent in bar 68; bar 1's f is editorial (in brackets).
**Not encodable:** the small natural printed above the trill sign in bars 116 and 118 (RH). The trills themselves are there.
**Print oddity:** bar 92 LH, beat 4: no natural is printed on the lower C; it is written C natural, as in the parallel bar 90.
**Fresh check:** four agents. All CLEAN on notes, rhythm, words and dynamics; the only finding was the two trill naturals above.
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters.
**Tempo:** quarter = 138 (bar 1), half = 108 (bar 129). Estimated (about 5 minutes); no track timing was found, as most recordings leave this aria out.
