**Layout:**
- The pickup "Muo-jo d'af-" is printed at the end of the recitative before it and is in that file, not here.
- Bar 27, the last Andante bar, is printed with 3 quarters before the double bar; it is set as 3/4.
- Bar 28 is the chorus's one-quarter pickup, set as 1/4. The chorus (Maestoso, D major) runs from bar 28 to 53.

- Sopranos and altos share one staff in the print. Where one notehead serves both, both parts sing it.

**Process:** each chunk had two readers, a judge and a marks sweep. The sung lines of the first chunk were identical in both readings. The chunks were then stitched together.
