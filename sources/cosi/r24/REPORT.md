# Recitativo "Che silenzio! che aspetto di tristezza", Peters pp. 87-91
SEGS `1/4/4/0/` · SINGERS `A:Don Alfonso:D.A.:F/4,E:Despina:De.:G/2`
**Bars:** 65, in two chunks: p. 87 system 3 to p. 89 (bars 1-40) and p. 90 to the double bar on p. 91 system 3 (bars 41-65).
**Layout:** bar 65 is printed short (one quarter, then the double bar and "attacca Nr. 13"); it is filled with rests to a full bar.
**Process:** each chunk had two readers and a judge (first chunk 10 X / 1 Y / 1 OWN; second 9 X / 5 Y). Then stitched.
**Italian rhythm:** used wherever the print gives a second rhythm for the German words (bars 12, 14, 27, 29, 36, 42, 44, 46, 56, 57, 60, 61).
**Print oddity:** bar 23, Alfonso's line is printed a 16th too long; the second B flat is shortened to a 16th.
**Fresh check:** one round, three errors, all fixed.
- Bars 42 and 44, Alfonso: "ro an-" and "le un" are each one eighth (the two 16ths are the German rhythm).
- Bar 15 RH: the inner E4 is tied into beat 3.

**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters ("vorebbe", "macchine", "quà", "Si" without accent).
**Tempo:** quarter = 93, from Rattle (2:37), Klemperer (2:47) and Jacobs (2:54).
