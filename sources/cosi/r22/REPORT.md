SEGS `1/4/4/0/;55/3/4/0/` · SINGERS `E:Despina:De.:G/2,D:Dorabella:Do.:G/2,F:Fiordiligi:Fi.:G/2`
**Bars:** 55, from p. 80 to the double bar on p. 83 system 1. Bar 55 is printed with only 3 quarters before "attacca subito Nr. 12"; it is set as 3/4.
**Process:** two readers, then a judge (1 X / 47 Y / 2 OWN; 28 of them were only how rests are grouped).
**Worth knowing:**
- Bars 32-34 are Dorabella's (she continues from bar 31); Despina enters on the last beat of bar 34.
- Italian rhythm is used where the print gives a second one for the German words (bars 9, 10, 18, 29, 31, 38, 44, 48, 49, 53).
- Bar 34 RH lower voice: D4 then A3, read from the ledger lines.

**Fresh check:** one round, CLEAN. One doubt left open: bar 15, "pe-rir." may be printed with a colon.
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters ("Guglielmo", "più tosto", "ven' han", "buon' ora", "perchè").
**Tempo:** quarter = 97, from Rattle (2:10) and Klemperer (2:21).
