# Recitativo "Non son cattivo comico", Peters pp. 69-70
SEGS `1/4/4/0/;14/4/4/0/Allegro moderato` · SINGERS `A:Don Alfonso:D.A.:F/4`
**Bars:** 26. Bars 1-13 are the secco recitative; bar 14 (p. 70) starts the Allegro moderato arioso "Nel mare solca".
**Process:** two readers. The lead judged the three sung differences on zoomed crops and by measurement (bar 4 "gna, e" is A3 and the syllables run "no at-ten-"; bar 12 "mo-re" is F3).
**Fresh check:** one round, CLEAN.
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters ("femmina" against the libretto's "femina").
**Tempo:** quarter = 91, from Rattle (1:03) and Klemperer (1:14), one tempo for the whole piece.
