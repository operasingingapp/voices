# Recitativo "Oh la saria da ridere", Peters pp. 144-147
SEGS `1/4/4/0/` · SINGERS `A:Don Alfonso:D.A.:F/4,E:Despina:De.:G/2`
**Bars:** 65, in two chunks: pp. 144-145 (bars 1-32) and pp. 146-147 (bars 33-65, to "segue Nr. 18 Finale").
**Process:** each chunk had two readers and a judge (first 5 X / 1 Y in the voices, 2 piano ties Y; second 2 X / 25 Y / 1 OWN). Then stitched.
**Print oddities:**
- Bar 10, Despina: printed a 16th short; the last note ("coll'") is lengthened to an eighth.
- Bar 45, Alfonso: printed a 16th short; "que-" is lengthened to an eighth.

**Kept as printed:** bar 5 "fun-" (the libretto has "fanciulla"), "at-ten-dan-do-mi", "can-ter-an" (bar 63), "puo" without accent.
**Italian rhythm:** used wherever the print gives a second rhythm for the German words.
**Fresh check:** one round, CLEAN.
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters.
**Tempo:** quarter = 99, from Jacobs (2:37).
