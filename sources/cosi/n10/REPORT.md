**Bars:** 41. Bars 39-41 are piano only. The print gives [Andante] in brackets.
**Process:** two readers, then a judge (20 X / 31 Y / 0 OWN; most were spelling of naturals), then a marks sweep (7 marks, 2 slur corrections).

- Bar 28: Alfonso's last note and the LH are B sharp. Bar 29: F double sharp.
- Bar 12: the small notes for Fiordiligi and Dorabella are grace notes.

**Fresh check:** one round, CLEAN.
**Words:** checked against the libretto (Digitale Mozart-Edition LV_311). Words that differ from it are kept as printed in Peters.
